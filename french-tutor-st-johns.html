Winny French helps St. John's students and adults build real French confidence online, with group classes from $25 per student per hour. St. John's students study French in core French and immersion, and online tutoring brings extra help right to your kitchen table. FAQ

St. John's French tutoring questions.

Do you tutor the French programs used in St. John's schools?

Yes. Our tutors support core French and French immersion students in Newfoundland and Labrador, and help with classwork, homework, and assessments at each grade level.

How much does a French tutor cost in St. John's?

Small group classes are $25 per student per hour, with up to three learners per class. One-on-one classes are $35 per hour, or $30 per hour each when a family takes more than one one-on-one class a week. Every learner can start with a free 30-minute trial.

Are the classes online or in person?

Classes are online over video call, so any St. John's family can join from home. Classes run Monday to Friday 4pm to 9pm Eastern, which is 5:30pm to 10:30pm in St. John's, and anytime on weekends.

Can you help adults in St. John's prepare for TEF Canada or TCF Canada?

Yes. We prepare adults for TEF Canada and TCF Canada, the French tests used for Canadian immigration, with practice across listening, reading, writing, and speaking. Tell us your target score and test date when you book your free trial.
